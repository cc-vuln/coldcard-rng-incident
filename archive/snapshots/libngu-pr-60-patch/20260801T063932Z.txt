reseed() assigned only yasmarang_pad from a 32-bit-truncated argument
and never touched n/d/dat, so after reseeding the whole generator was a
function of a single 32-bit word (a 2**32 state space). When the on-chip
TRNG is biased or backdoored this reseed is the only independent entropy,
so its width is the wallet's real security margin.
Accept a bytes-like seed and absorb every byte into all state words with
per-byte diffusion; keep accepting a plain int for backward compatibility.
Extend test_random.py to exercise full-width (digest) seeding.
    Four low bytes of chance,
    the whole seed narrowed to noise —
    now the full digest.
