Crypto Hacks: Report and Stay Safe with Chainabuse
1

My Coldcard Mk3 hardware wallet was compromised as part of a publicly disclosed firmware vulnerability (RNG/entropy flaw in Coldcard firmware versions 4.0.1–4.1.9, disclosed by Coinkite on July 30, 2026). The weak entropy made it possible for an attacker to derive private keys for wallets generated on affected firmware. My seed was generated on this device in approximately December 2021.




On July 31, 2026 at approximately 07:34 UTC, three unauthorized transactions swept approximately 0.32531190 BTC from my wallet without my authorization:

All three transactions sent funds to the same destination address: bc1qsjrf5ze5tmulz7y2x4pc7qaex2a35sanp3rqlx, which appears to function as a collection address — it received funds from many transactions in rapid succession consistent with a mass sweep of multiple victims' wallets, then forwarded the funds onward shortly after.




This appears to be part of a broader incident affecting hundreds of Coldcard wallets, with total reported losses in the tens of millions of dollars. I have filed reports with the FBI's IC3 and my local sheriff's department.
