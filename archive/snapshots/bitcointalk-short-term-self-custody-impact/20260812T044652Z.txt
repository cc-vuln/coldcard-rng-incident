Title: Re: Short-term impact of the Coldcard incident on Self-Custody
Post by: MadSl1m on August 08, 2026, 08:19:51 AM
Worth reading the vendor advisory before anyone in this thread decides anything about custody models, because one line in it is the actionable part: updating the firmware does not repair an existing seed.
https://blog.coinkite.com/coldcard-mk3-seed-generation-warning/
The failure is in seed generation, not in anyone getting access to a device. Mk2 and Mk3 firmware 4.0.1 through 4.1.9 are affected, and seeds made on Mk4, Mk5 and Q before the fixed releases carry roughly 72 bits of entropy where 128 was expected. Coinkite's exception is narrow: at least 50 fair, independent, private dice rolls at seed creation, and they don't consider that seed at risk from this issue alone. A strong unique BIP-39 passphrase lowers the exposure but leaves the seed as it is.
Which is also the answer to the OP. The guarantee that broke was device-level, not custody-level. Moving the same seed to an exchange fixes none of that. It just puts a counterparty on top of it.
