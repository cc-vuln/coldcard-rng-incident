This improves PR #707’s mash method by hashing microsecond-resolution timing captured at the raw key edge, before debounce and UI delays, instead of relying on coarse processed key events and key-choice distribution. Following Peter Todd’s push-button RNG model, it conservatively credits one timing bit per press, requires 128 presses, and hashes each complete timing interval while remaining safe even when repeatedly pressing one key.
