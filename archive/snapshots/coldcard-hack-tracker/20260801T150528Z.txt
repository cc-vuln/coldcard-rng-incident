Am I at risk?

Short checklist from Coinkite’s Aug 1 advisory and Block’s root-cause writeup. This tracker does not check your wallet — follow the official steps before moving funds.

LIKELY EXPOSED

Your seed is likely exposed if it was generated on vulnerable firmware. Fixed releases and later are safe for new seeds.

DEVICE	VULNERABLE	FIXED
Mk3	4.0.1–4.1.9 (~40-bit)	4.2.0+
Mk4 / Mk5	Before Aug 1 patch (~72-bit)	5.6.0+
Q	Before Aug 1 patch (~72-bit)	1.5.0Q+
Edge (Mk4 / Mk5)	Before Aug 1 patch	6.6.0X+
Edge (Q)	Before Aug 1 patch	6.6.0QX+
FIRMWARE UPDATE IS NOT ENOUGH
Patching does not repair an existing seed. Generate a new seed on fixed firmware and migrate funds carefully.
BEYOND THE MAIN SEED
The same weak RNG also covered paper-wallet keys, Seed XOR masks, and some Key Teleport / clone / Secure Notes material (Block).
LOWER RISK
At least 50 private dice rolls at seed creation, or a strong unique BIP-39 passphrase (not the device PIN). Still migrate when practical.
NOT THIS BUG
TAPSIGNER, Opendime, and Satscard use different codebases and are not affected.
