Title: Re: Bitcoin ETF Inflows Surge Following $130M Coldcard Hack
Post by: Smack That Ace on August 06, 2026, 08:54:39 PM
You may have confused correlation with causation.
It is not logical to assume that inflow into ETF after Coldcard hack means that people are abandoning self custody and buying ETFs.
ETF flows change every day for variety of reasons. We saw large inflow due to institutional asset allocation, macro sentiment, treasury management, arbitrage and even basis trade. So, if two event happen at same time, it is not right to assume that one caused for other.
If a bank vault has a flaw, would you say the fiat system has failed?
Similarly, it is unreasonable to call self-custody a failure based on an implementation bug in a hardware wallet. But yes, if some new users choose ETFs out of fear, that is possible. But it is not right to think that the entire $626 million inflow is the result of that fear.
