If you use a single-sig Coldcard-generated seed, particularly without sufficient external entropy or a strong passphrase, you should migrate your funds ASAP to a fresh seed / address that was not generated by the Coldcard. This attacker will not be the last. Eventually, any remaining addresses generated by Coldcards since 2021 will be drained. 

Note that if you use Coldcard in a multisig or collaborative custody quorum and Coldcard(s) alone cannot reached signature threshold, your funds are safe. You should still rotate the Coldcard-generated keys in such setups for good hygiene.
