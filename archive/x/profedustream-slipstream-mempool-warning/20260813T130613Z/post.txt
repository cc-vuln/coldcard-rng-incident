Liana and multi-sig users:

 If one of the keys securing your wallet has been generated on an affected Coldcard, then broadcasting your transaction to Bitcoin’s public mempool could give an attacker an opportunity to replace it with a competing transaction and steal your funds.

To reduce this risk, the 
@lianabitcoin
 team has developed a tool that submits your signed transaction privately to MARA’s Slipstream service, rather than broadcasting it to the public mempool:

https://
outofband.wizardsardine.com

 Here is a step-by-step tutorial explaining how to use this tool with Liana or others compatible multi-sig wallets, like Sparrow.
It is available in 28 languages:

https://
planb.academy/en/tutorials/w
allet/desktop/slipstream-3d024596-70b8-4161-96c8-eb5bea8f3228

 Important: this is not suitable for every emergency. If your seed is already at critical risk of theft, submitting the transaction to a single miner may be too slow.
