OPENDIME appears to be incorporating entropy. Its firmware predated the issue with the Cold Card, so this makes sense to me.

The Tap Signer is closed source, and as such we are unable to verify the state of the entropy generation first hand.

Some preliminary work from 
@otaliptus
 (who has been incredibly instrumental in the intitial analysis of the cold card issue) points to it not using the libngu library which impacted the cold card, but the full code is not accessible for us to evaluate.

I have always viewed the tapsigner as a spending wallet key with a phone. Even before the past 48 hours, I would never have recommended it for large sums of bitcoin.

I have spoken with multiple people today who are using Tap Signers as a signer in their large stacks. While there is no known active issue with the Tap Signer, If it were my bitcoin or my family's bitcoin wealth, I would out of an abundance of caution work on getting those funds moved.
