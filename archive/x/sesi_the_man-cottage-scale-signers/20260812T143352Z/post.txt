It's a little different b/c what happened with CC was a software bug and what you're alluding to is a supply chain attack. But the point stands and this is why I've always said building signers should be a cottage scale thing and people selling them should have a solid reputation in their local bitcoin community. Also, realistically I think the bigger risk is people being attacked via pre-loaded software (this is why I've been emphatic that sellers should never include software with shipments) and misled via malicious documentation that comes with the signer (bad download link, "here is your seed phrase", etc. etc.). But still, and again as I've always said, the best way to get a SeedSigner is to build one yourself.
