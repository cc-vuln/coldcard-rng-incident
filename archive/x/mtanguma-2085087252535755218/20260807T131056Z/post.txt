Bitcoin custody has become absurd to everyone.

Just talked to a guy today who is Bitcoin-only, uses Coldcard, runs Sparrow, understands the whole thing.

And he said the quiet part everyone is now thinking:

"rolling dice 100 times and expecting his wife to inherit that setup is preposterous."

Exactly.

People found Bitcoin because they wanted peace of mind.

Then the industry handed them seed phrases, metal plates, firmware risk, inheritance risk, device risk, duress risk, and told them they were the problem if they didn’t want to become security engineers.

I don’t buy it. Never have. 

Self-custody matters.

But serious wealth needs serious infrastructure.

Not one exchange.

Not one hardware wallet.

Not one spouse trying to figure out Sparrow in the worst week of her life after someone passes.

That’s the whole point of Multi-Institution Custody.

Secured by multiple institutions.

Controlled by the client.

Built so one bad thing doesn’t end the game.

We all need to level up.
