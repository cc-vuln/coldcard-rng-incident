COLDCARD ATTACKERS BEGIN ATTEMPTING TO LAUNDER STOLEN BITCOIN AS LARGEST HAUL REMAINS FROZEN

Galaxy Research says the largest known COLDCARD theft, 1,159 BTC spread across seven attacker addresses, remains untouched, with 0 BTC cashed out or sent through mixers. 

The coins were stolen in just 41 minutes, but spending them has proven far more difficult as roughly 600 attacker addresses have been flagged by law enforcement, exchanges, and blockchain analytics firms.

Meanwhile, a smaller attacker appears to have begun laundering funds. 

On-chain analysts traced 64 BTC entering a mixer, with only about 10 BTC mixed initially while 54 BTC returned as change before being split into roughly 7 BTC outputs for additional mixing. 

Analysts say the unusually large outputs remain easily traceable, making the laundering attempt relatively transparent.
