Nunchuk users: we heard you loud and clear.

We’re actively adding new inheritance-key options to our off-chain timelock protocol.

The off-chain protocol needs the hardware signer to support on-device encryption: the user encrypts the inheritance key, and only at the right time can the heir decrypt and claim, so funds are never released early. Today that’s Tapsigner and Coldcard. The 
@BlockstreamJade
 and 
@FoundationHQ
 teams have confirmed they’re adding encryption support. Kudos and thanks to both for the quick response.

For our on-chain timelock inheritance protocol, you can already use 
@BlockstreamJade
 and 
@Ledger
 (and soon BitBox02) for the inheritance key.

We’ve also reached out to 
@Ledger
 and 
@BitBoxSwiss
 about device encryption. (If anyone has a 
@Trezor
 contact, let us know.)
