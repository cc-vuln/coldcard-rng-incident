I'm trying to wrap my hear around the game theory of the attacks, while the MK3s without passphrases are all completely drained (which i guess is the case).

Given how quickly the community reacted and people are moving their funds, the REWARD for the attackers is going down, while their COST stays the same. This is a good deterrent to demotivate further drain. (mk4 and co, passphrases)

This reward is going down, so the urgency for the attackers is high. We aren't talking about GPUs at home anymore, but spending millions in GPU rental costs to grind as much as possible, as quickly as possible.
Is this worth it still? Is there still more money sitting on MK3, MK3+weak passphrases, or MK4+ than the cost to break them quick?

Another layer to this is that multiple attackers is a diminishing return for them, although not linearly. Are there many large scale attackers out there currently?

I have NO CLUE how much money could still be in weak wallets (assuming the weakest would now be Mk3+ very weak passphrase).

Optimizations are probably still possible to reduce the range of the PRNG entropy, making things cheaper to break. But not "cheap".
