The Coldcard attack is terrible for bitcoin privacy 

Because seed phrases are being compromised - attackers can see all the transaction history and addresses that belong to a particular wallet - even if you moved the funds off that wallet

This is bad for everyone else’s privacy too: 
- surveillance companies will probably group addresses together and assign them to 1 owner 
- everyone else hides in a smaller set of unknown addresses

Think of it like hiding in a room of 1000 people. Suddenly 800 leave. 

You are now hiding in a much smaller room and are easier to identify
