After a full dive into Mk4 today:
Mk4 keys are not enumerable like Mk3 keys at all. Thanks to the reseed, it’s basically impossible to bruteforce them even, if they have weak entropy.
When correctly set up, the private keys are so secure that they remain effectively uncrackable, even if their true entropy turns out to be lower than expected.
