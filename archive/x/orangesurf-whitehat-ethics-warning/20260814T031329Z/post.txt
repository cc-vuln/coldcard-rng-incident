The ethics of this are very murky, we are are in unexplored territory here and I anticipate it will create a mountain of problems for the whitehat.

A theft to prevent theft with no known mechanism to return the coin feels wrong, but at least theres a chance that the coins get returned (e.g. with a physical device it may be possible to provide a high degree of confidence of ownership). On the other hand, it's unknowable whether the coins would have been migrated by the owner in time.

To anyone doing this, at least include an OP_RETURN with a link to a site to provide information to recover the funds which clearly says to preserve the physical device and provides contact information.
