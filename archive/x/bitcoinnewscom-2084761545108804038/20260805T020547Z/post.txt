BITCOIN RED TEAM FINDS DOZENS OF CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES AS MASSIVE AI-POWERED SECURITY PUSH EXPANDS

The volunteer Bitcoin Red Team says it has scanned 150 repositories, privately disclosed more than a dozen vulnerabilities, and is finding critical issues at an alarming rate as it races to harden wallets, cryptographic libraries, and infrastructure following the COLDCARD exploit.

The effort has already consumed roughly $20,000 in AI compute, with OpenSats covering costs. 

Researchers are using multiple AI harnesses, with many saying open-source models like Kimi K3 have become the primary workhorse for security research, while frontier closed models such as OpenAI and Anthropic are viewed as more constrained for this type of analysis.

The team plans to open source its AI security harness so projects can continuously audit their own codebases.

Researchers warn Bitcoin may simply be the first industry facing a wave of AI-assisted vulnerability discovery before similar efforts spread across all open source software.
