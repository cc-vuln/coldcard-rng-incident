Independent Coldcard attackers are draining victims in the ongoing campaign. Their distinct on-chain strategies distinguish them. Chainalysis is monitoring the following, and more:

Type 1: The first wave of attacks: Victim wallets →  attacker wallet → consolidation wallet, where $35M still sits dormant. The attacker here appears to be waiting it out.

Type 2: Some attackers are moving stolen funds cross-chain and then into mixing services like TornadoCash. 

Type 3: Other attackers are moving funds through intermediary addresses and then onward to centralized exchanges, incurring significant risk.

Type 4: Still others have pushed funds into mixing services like Wasabi Wallet, attempting to obfuscate the flow of funds.

All of these strategies leave clues for investigators following the money with Chainalysis.
