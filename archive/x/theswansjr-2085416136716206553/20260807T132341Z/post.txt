Some are calling the Coldcard hack an obituary for self-custody.
I think it will make self-custody even stronger.

A five-year-old entropy bug was exploited. Roughly $90 million was stolen.
Brutal.
Real.

All visible on-chain for the entire world to audit in real time.
Now compare that to the traditional financial system.

When a bank gets breached, the report gets buried, the lawyers show up, a bailout follows, and the losses are ultimately socialized through inflation.

When Coldcard got breached, the network identified it within hours.
A fix shipped within days.

The exploit didn't expose a flaw in self-custody.
It exposed a flaw in one specific product.

The network learns.
Users adapt.
Products improve.

That's what antifragile systems do.
They don't die from attacks.
They absorb them and come back stronger.
