Apr 12, 2024 -- "Hey \n@foundationhq\n, ya'll planning to enable dice roll seed generation anytime soon?"

--- post text (verbatim) ---

So far we've made a conscious decision to not add dice rolls. We'd much prefer that users pull seed words out of a hat and allow Passport to autocomplete the 12th or 24th word.

Numerous users have lost funds on Coldcard in the last several months due to rolling a low number of dice rolls (their team has deleted posts on Reddit and blocked users who reported these problems).  Even enforcing a minimum limit on dice rolls does not add assurance; for example a user could mash random numbers on the keypad which would lead to poor entropy.

There's no easy way for us to assess the entropy of the seed. We could preprogram Passport with a "blacklist" of seeds by brute forcing low-entropy seeds, but that would only be an incomplete solution.

Additionally if the firmware is malicious, there are other ways it could extricate seed words. Dice rolls do not fully protect against malicious firmware.

So the only thing they do protect against is a mistake in the entropy generation of the hardware wallet – but our firmware has been audited and has been in the wild for 3+ years, and we combine multiple sources of entropy – including an avalanche noise source, which is a fully open source circuit (the only of its kind in a hardware wallet). 

Also our firmware is fully open source and reproducible.

I hope this gives you more context. Please let us know if you have further questions and we appreciate any feedback.
