Following the recent Coldcard incident, Nick Neuman (
@Nneuman
), CEO of 
@CasaHODL
, explains how Bitcoiners can build a more resilient self-custody setup by eliminating single points of failure:

"I think that there's still a lot of potential for self-custody for the future of humanity."

"People really need to shift their mindset when they are self-custodying their assets. You need to figure out a setup where multiple different providers, companies, security experts, are acting as a check on each other. And if one of them fails, your whole setup doesn't fail."
