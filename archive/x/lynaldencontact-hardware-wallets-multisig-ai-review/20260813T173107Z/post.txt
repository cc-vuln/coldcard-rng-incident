In response to this other company's recent design failure, several within the bitcoin community are already performing AI-assisted analysis of hundreds of code repositories to look for bugs, and identifying a ton for responsible disclosure.

Shocks to a decentralized ecosystem help make it stronger. With the rise of AI, every latent vulnerability in existing and future software needs to be analyzed with white hat AI for defense.
