210,000 BTC MOVES AMID COLDCARD FALLOUT

Roughly 210,000 BTC worth $13.6 billion moved from long-term holder wallets over the past week amid the fallout from the COLDCARD security vulnerability.

On-chain analysts say the movement appears to be custody migration rather than capitulation, with Bitcoin moving into newly secured self-custody setups and regulated custodians, including spot ETFs.

Long-term holder supply fell from 15.0 million BTC to 14.7 million BTC, while Bitcoin remained near $64,700, suggesting reallocation rather than widespread selling.
