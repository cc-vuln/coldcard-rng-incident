cktripwire now has 2 live multisig honeypots:
- 2-of-3 revealed: two of the keys are bad-cc, no entropy added, and pubkeys have been revealed via spend
- 3-of-3 dark: all three keys are bad-cc, no entropy added

The 3-of-3 is particularly interesting because *if* it is swept, it will tell us that attackers are proactively constructing multisig scripts on the basis of pubkeys they've never seen, just based off of speculatively combining iterations of the weak entropy.

Consequently, it has a juicy UTXO value attached to it.
