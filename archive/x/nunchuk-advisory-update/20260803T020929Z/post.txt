Update on the Coldcard advisory, based on Block’s analysis (link below):

1/ Scope is wider than the initial Mk3 advisory. Mk4, Q, and Mk5 also appear affected. The secure-element reseed adds only 32 bits, which is brute-forceable. Any seed generated on-device on a Coldcard since 2021 should be treated as suspect until Coinkite’s full report.

2/ Refining our multisig guidance: one compromised key still can’t move funds, but a quorum made up entirely of affected Coldcard seeds does not have that protection. If your multisig keys were all generated on post-2021 Coldcards, rotate enough keys to restore a secure quorum, sooner rather than later.

Seeds imported into a Coldcard are not affected. Dice-roll seeds are safe if enough rolls were used (99+).
