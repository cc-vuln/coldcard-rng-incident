Bitcoiners protecting each other is the fundamental mission of Unchained and collaborative custody.  We believe that combining devices from multiple manufacturers in multi-party, multisig reduces your risk of being exposed to a single manufacturer or counterparty's vulnerabilities.  This is why we added Coldcard support to begin with and why this tragedy only reinforces our belief in our mission.

Because we built Unchained precisely for moments like this, most Unchained clients are unaffected by this vulnerability.  Yet it's been all-hands-on-deck at Unchained since yesterday. I know it's the same at every other bitcoin company right now.

We’re doing everything we can to alert our Coldcard-using clients about their risk level and advise them through the steps required for mitigation.  If you're an  Unchained client, you may see emails, phone calls, blog posts and in-app messages related to this issue.  Please pay attention to these!

**NOTE:** Moments of urgency such as this are often used by spearphishers to trick you.  If you get a phone call or email from someone claiming to be from Unchained make sure you use our support PIN feature to verify their identity.
