My mind is still blown thinking about how white hat sweeps could've potentially been returned to victims if Bitcoin had a robust DID ecosystem with decentralized social graphs helping arbitrate recovery.

Imagine a white hat sweeping vulnerable wallets and locking the funds into a recovery contract. Anyone could claim ownership by proving control of their DID...but if the DID were derived from the compromised key, so could the attacker.

That's where the social graph comes in. Your pre-registered trusted contacts on a decentralized network like Nostr could attest to your identity and approve releasing the funds to a brand new wallet you provide out of band.

No government, no law enforcement, no lawyers, no middlemen taking a cut. Just a trust-minimized recovery process built on cryptographic identity and the people who actually know you.

And for funds recovered by law enforcement, the same recovery framework could be used if they chose to.

I hope we build this kind of crazy decentralized ecosystem on top of Bitcoin one day.
