Finally, I want to address my colleagues at Coinkite, other hardware wallet manufacturers, bitcoin companies, and the tech community more broadly: this bug sucks, and the consequences are scary, but I'm not *surprised* to see a zero day like this manifest in the wild.

The layers of software we've inherited and built further-upon over the decades are full of undiscovered zero days like this one. Terrible monsters emerging from the melting permafrost of cyberspace, exposed by the scouring light of of some attacker with an LLM.

Bitcoin, AI and computer security are overlapping concerns and we technologists need to be better prepared for what is to come. The main solutions I see, that I practice, are paranoia, layers of complementary protections, redundancy, and humans in the loop. What are you doing?
