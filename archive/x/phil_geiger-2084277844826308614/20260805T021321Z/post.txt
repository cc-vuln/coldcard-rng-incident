Some thoughts on multisig: 

You're moving into an inherently more complicated form of custody, so the devices you select should make your life as easy as possible. I optimize on UX and functionality: 


@Ledger
: Best form factor and UX in the industry, and great multisig functionality. 


@BlockstreamJade
: setup is a little clunky and I feel like the device has a tough time working consistently well with the mobile app/desktop app for updates, but once everything is setup, it's dead simple and has great multisig functionality


@BitBoxSwiss
: very quick and easy setup. The form factor is a bit too small, feels cheaper than the other devices, and it's overall slightly tougher to use although still way easier than a Coldcard. Great multisig functionality, but can't support P2SH multisig. 

Honorable mention: 

@Trezor
: Great singlesig UX and form factor. I really, really wish they'd improve their multisig functionality and let users register the wallet on the device. Currently multisig is clunky on the trezor. Also, BIP39 should be the default when setting up the device.
