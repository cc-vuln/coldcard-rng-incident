If 93,000 seeds were generated via COLDCARD, there’s a 50% chance two people would generate the same 24 word phrase / wallet

With proper entropy, a 24-word seed phrase has 256 bits of randomness 

(2^256 possible seeds, about 1.16 × 10^77).

That’s 400 undecillion wallets
 400,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000.

If all 8 billion humans generated a billion seeds per second, it would take about 1.6 trillion years to reach 50% collision odds.
