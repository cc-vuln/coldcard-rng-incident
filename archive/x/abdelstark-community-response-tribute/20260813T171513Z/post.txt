this effort started within the bitcoin ecosystem in response to the coldcard incident is one of the most beautiful evidence of what makes bitcoin so special and unique. 

the situation is unprecedented and we are entering scary times, cybersecurity as we knew it is gone, it’s an entire new world where your protection is proportional to the amount of tokens / compute you are willing to spend.

this changes the equation radically. seeing so many bitcoiners contributing days and nights, and helping affected users, in a benevolent manner, is beautiful. 

those toxic bitcoin maxis are having sleepless nights and are dedicating their time, energy and even money to help others.
