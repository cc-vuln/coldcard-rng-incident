NOTICE: CLAIMS THAT CURRENT COLDCARD FIRMWARE PERMANENTLY BRICKS DEVICES ARE INCORRECT

Developers say there is no evidence the current TRNG patch permanently bricks COLDCARDs.

If a transient TRNG seed error occurs, a full power cycle (completely remove power, then restart) should recover the device under normal hardware conditions. The error is volatile, nothing is written to flash, and the firmware is designed to fail closed.

A new proposed patch further hardens RNG recovery by following ST’s documented recovery procedure, including bounded retries, seed-error detection, and discarding stale RNG output after recovery. Mk3 support is being handled in a separate follow-up PR.
