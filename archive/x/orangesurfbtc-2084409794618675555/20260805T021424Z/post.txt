Dice rolls are not ridiculous. 

Someone with basic technical competency (can use a computer, can follow instructions, can't verify source code) can easily verify that dice rolls are being used by their signing device to derive their test seed/wallet/first address by inputting the  test rolls into a second, different device (e.g. their computer on 
https://
bitcoiner.guide/seed/#/seed). 

Once they are confident that indeed the device is correctly converting their entropy into a seed deterministically, they can repeat for their real seed without entering it anywhere else. 

Even a poor quality dice will provide sufficient entropy with enough rolls. I am doing a deep dive into this at the moment, but currently I am confident that with 100 rolls of any normal dice you buy off the shelf is going to provide sufficient entropy (128 bits). If you are concerned about uncertain dice quality you can simply recommend more rolls (e.g. 150).
