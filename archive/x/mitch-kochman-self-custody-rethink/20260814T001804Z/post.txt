Yesterday we announced the biggest pricing change in 
@AnchorWatch
’s history.

Custody now starts at $100/month for up to $500k of bitcoin, and for the large holders we’ve capped our maximum fee at $1,000/month, no matter how much bitcoin you hold.

We made this change because I don’t think cost should determine whether someone has access to the custody solution that’s right for them.

While I believe the option to self custody is fundamental to Bitcoin, the 
@COLDCARD
 exploit, what it actually took to bring the device entropy, and reinforced that it shouldn’t be the default answer for everyone.

Since I entered the industry, I’ve believed we’ve done individuals a disservice by not providing them with the same quality custody options available to institutions. The best single party custodians have largely been reserved for institutions, leaving everyone else to choose between leaving assets on an exchange, taking on the responsibility of self custody, using an assisted custody solution, or simply buying the ETF.

I think that’s prevented a lot of people from owning bitcoin.

Bitcoin gives us the ability to build custody that is resilient to failures like this. With Multi-Institution Custody, security is distributed across independent institutions with separate infrastructure. Your security no longer depends on any single company, device, or implementation getting everything right.

I’m incredibly proud that AnchorWatch built this product, and even more proud that we’re making it accessible to everyone. I want the best custody tools in the industry to be available to individuals and small businesses, not just institutions.

If this week’s events have you rethinking your custody, my DMs are open. Whether you become an AnchorWatch customer or not, my team and I are happy to help you think through your options and find the approach that’s right for you and your family.
