If you are sweeping a multisig wallet, this lets you paste a PSBT to have your transaction mined without publishing descriptor information prior to confirmation.

This may be useful in situations where you have coins in a multisig where the number of keys generated on a Coldcard meet the threshold for spending, but the attackers currently bruteforcing keys are missing information to reconstruct your scripts (addresses).

It goes without saying that it's terribly weak security, but the information asymmetry here may be enough to avoid getting your funds stolen.
