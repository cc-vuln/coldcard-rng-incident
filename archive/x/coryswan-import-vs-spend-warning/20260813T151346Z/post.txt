One important and somewhat subtle issue raised on Spaces:

If a person imported their cold card wallet into a different hardware device vs sending/spending their bitcoin to the new wallet over the network, they're still vulnerable!

They might have done this in the past to save Tx fees or due to loss/breakage/theft of the cold card.

Their new wallet is then just as vulnerable because the seed is low entropy since it was generated on the flawed CC.

I suspect that some people when they hear the CCs are broken, might do this kind of import process thinking that it will help. It will not!

A new seed should be generated on the new HW wallet and the BTC spent into it.
