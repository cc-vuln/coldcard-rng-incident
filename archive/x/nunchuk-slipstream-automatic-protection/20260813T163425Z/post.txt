IMPORTANT UPDATE Part 2: Using Slipstream to safely migrate funds off multisig wallets containing Coldcard(s)

For context on the Coldcard seed-generation vulnerability and your wallet migration options, see our previous posts.

Our v2.7.2 iOS release is still pending Apple review, but our engineers found a way to make automatic Slipstream protection work on the existing iOS app (v2.7.1) in the meantime. So all platforms are now covered.

Make sure you’re on the latest version before migrating:
•  iOS: v2.7.1 (already in the App Store)
•  Android: v2.7.5
•  Desktop: v2.6.4

Once updated, any transaction you broadcast from an assisted multisig wallet containing at least one Coldcard is automatically routed through Slipstream. No extra steps.

To replace an affected Coldcard key, use the built-in Key Replacement feature in the mobile app:

Wallet > More menu > View wallet config > More menu > Replace key

Or create a new assisted wallet from scratch, using the extra assisted wallet we provisioned for your migration, at no extra cost.

Two things to know:
If you’re a free Nunchuk user, or a subscriber who connects to your own Electrum server: automatic routing won’t apply, and you’ll need the manual Slipstream submission method detailed in our previous post.

Slipstream transactions confirm more slowly, since MARA has only ~5-6% of total hash rate, and they won’t appear in the usual block explorers while pending. To check status, use the Slipstream page (replace TXID with your transaction ID):
