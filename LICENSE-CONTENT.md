# Content and data license

This repository holds three kinds of material under three different terms.
The split exists because the project can only license what it created.

## 1. Software: MIT

Everything under `scripts/` and `site/` and the build tooling. See
[LICENSE](LICENSE).

## 2. Original content and data: CC BY 4.0

The project's own writing and data are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0):

- editorial pages and their text as published on cc-vuln.org
- documentation, design documents and research notes in this repository
- diagrams and figures created by the project
- project-created data: `sources.toml`, `revision-reviews.toml`, capture
  metadata, hashes, the event index and generated registers

You may copy, mirror, adapt and redistribute this material, including
commercially, provided you attribute "cc-vuln.org" with a link. Full terms:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Attribution matters here beyond credit: this site's claims are graded and
linked to evidence, and stripping attribution breaks the chain a reader
needs to check them.

## 3. Archived third-party material: not ours to license

Captured snapshots, extracted text, diffs of third-party pages, and captured
media (everything under `archive/`) remain the copyright of their original
authors. This project holds them for research, archival and documentation
purposes, and its published output quotes them as excerpts with attribution.
The published site displays screenshots of short, public, attributed social
posts under the same rationale, with attribution and a link to the original,
and honours removal requests from their authors. Media attached to those
posts, material a source has asked to be withheld (`withhold_text` in the
registry), and anything identifying a private individual are not published
at all.

Nothing in this repository grants you any right to republish that material
beyond what its authors or applicable law allow. Links indexed by the
project imply no license at all.
